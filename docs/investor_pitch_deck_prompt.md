# ICONA - Investor Pitch Deck Generation Prompt

**Purpose:** A single, self-contained brief you can paste into any AI deck
generator (Gamma, Claude, GPT, Gemini, Beautiful.ai) *or* hand to a designer
to produce an investor-grade pitch deck for **ICONA** - the ERP + CRM for
construction SMEs, starting in Pakistan.

It is written to clear the bar for **YC and top European accelerators**
(Techstars, EF, Antler, Seedcamp). Every market figure is real and sourced
(see the Sources section - keep the citations, investors check).

**How to use this file:**
1. Read "The 30-second thesis" and "Narrative arc" so you hold the story.
2. Paste "The master prompt" into your generator to get a first full draft.
3. Use the "Slide-by-slide blueprint" to refine each slide's content.
4. Pull hard numbers from the "Market data appendix" - never invent figures.
5. Apply "Design direction" for on-brand visuals.
6. Fill every `[[FILL: ...]]` placeholder before you send it to anyone.

> **Two rules that matter more than anything else in this file:**
> (1) Investors fund *lines going up*, not features. Lead with the market and
> the wedge, prove you can win it, show the path to a big outcome.
> (2) Never state traction you don't have. Where you lack a number, use the
> honest framing given in each slide (pilot, LOI, waitlist, design-partner).

## The 30-second thesis (the one paragraph everything else supports)

> Construction is one of the world's largest industries and its least
> digitized. In Pakistan - a market of **240M+ people, 5M+ SMEs, and a
> construction sector that is the country's second-largest employer** -
> contractors still run multi-million-rupee projects on Excel, WhatsApp, and
> memory. The result is the same everywhere: **the vast majority of projects
> run over budget or behind schedule.** Global ERPs (SAP, Oracle, Procore)
> are built for enterprises, priced in dollars, and useless to a 15-person
> firm in Lahore. ICONA is the **first mobile-first, localized, affordable
> ERP + CRM built for these firms** - one connected chain from BOQ and
> budgeting to labour, subcontractors, and a live cash ledger, starting at
> **$25/month**. The wedge is SaaS; the prize is becoming the **system of
> record for an entire underserved industry** - and, on top of that data, the
> rails for its payments and financing.

## Narrative arc (the emotional + logical spine, in order)

1. **Huge, broken, invisible market.** Construction is enormous and the least
   digitized major industry on earth. In Pakistan it's the #2 employer, run
   almost entirely on paper and spreadsheets.
2. **The pain is universal and quantified.** ~98% of construction projects run
   over budget or late; large projects average ~79% cost overrun. Fragmented
   tools are the cause: estimates in Excel, actuals in WhatsApp, cash in
   someone's head.
3. **Incumbents can't serve this customer.** SAP/Oracle/Procore are built and
   priced for enterprises. The real "competitor" is the spreadsheet - which
   means the market is wide open, not saturated.
4. **We built the thing that fits.** One connected chain, mobile-first,
   localized, $25/month. A contractor is running their first project in a week.
5. **Why now is real, not rhetorical.** Pakistan just crossed the smartphone +
   4G + broadband threshold that makes on-site software possible, and SaaS is
   now a funded local category.
6. **The market is big enough to matter.** Bottoms-up: even a fraction of
   Pakistan's construction SMEs is a multi-million-ARR business; the same
   playbook extends across South Asia, MENA, and Africa.
7. **The wedge becomes a platform.** Own the system of record, then own the
   payments and the financing on top of it. That's the venture-scale outcome.
8. **We're the team to do it, and here's the proof we're moving.**
9. **Here's the ask and exactly what it buys.**

## Business model & unit economics (talking points)

- **Tiered SaaS with forced-upgrade limits:** Starter $25 (5 projects / 3
  members), Growth $50 (25 / 15), Enterprise $75 (unlimited). You never pay
  for headroom; you upgrade when you actually grow. This makes expansion
  revenue structural, not hoped-for.
- **Annual incentive:** pay 10 months, get 12 - improves cash and retention.
- **Localized billing:** USD list price, PKR bank-transfer billing - removes
  the #1 friction for local SMEs.
- **Low-touch onboarding:** live on the first project within a week -> low CAC,
  fast time-to-value.
- **Stickiness:** once the BOQ, ledger, and labour history live in ICONA, the
  switching cost is the whole business memory -> low churn, high LTV.
- **The expansion thesis:** every firm on ICONA is a future payments and
  lending customer. `[[FILL: model your CAC, gross margin %, payback months,
  and net revenue retention target here.]]`

## Future updates / roadmap (the investable vision)

This is what converts "useful tool" into "venture-scale platform." Structure
it as three horizons; investors should see the data moat compounding into
fintech.

### Horizon 1 - Deepen the ERP (make it indispensable) - 0-12 months
- **ICONA AI Copilot** (already in Enterprise tier): natural-language
  reporting ("show me every project over budget"), BOQ auto-pricing from
  historical project data, early cost-overrun prediction, Urdu voice input for
  site logs.
- **Workspace calendar + scheduling** with a sync engine (in progress).
- **Integrations**: WhatsApp/Telegram notifications, accounting export, Excel
  round-trips - meet firms where they already work.
- **Offline-first Android** for low-connectivity sites (sync when back online).
- **Full localization**: Urdu UI, right-to-left support, local number/date
  formats.
- **Outcome for investors:** deeper product = lower churn + higher ARPU;
  every logged project enriches the proprietary cost dataset.

### Horizon 2 - Embedded payments (own the money movement) - 12-24 months
- **Integrated invoicing** straight from the ledger - the client portal
  already shows the numbers; add "pay now."
- **Local payment rails**: Raast (state instant-payment), JazzCash, Easypaisa,
  bank transfer - earn a **transaction take-rate** on top of subscriptions.
- **Vendor/subcontractor payouts** run through ICONA.
- **FBR e-invoicing & tax compliance** - become the compliant record of truth,
  a powerful retention and expansion hook as documentation tightens.
- **Outcome for investors:** revenue per customer stops being capped by the
  subscription; take-rate scales with the customer's business volume.

### Horizon 3 - Embedded finance & marketplace (own the industry's rails) - 24-48 months
- **Working-capital / project financing** underwritten on ICONA's own ledger
  and payment data - a **data moat no generic lender has**. This is the
  venture-scale prize: fintech margins on a SaaS-acquired customer base, in
  the most-funded category in Pakistani VC.
- **Materials & subcontractor marketplace**: connect firms already on the
  platform; procurement, quotes, and payments in one place.
- **Benchmarking & insights**: anonymized cost/productivity benchmarks sold
  back to the industry.
- **Regional expansion**: the same "informal, mobile-first, underserved
  construction SME" profile exists across **Bangladesh, MENA, and Africa** -
  replicate the playbook.
- **Outcome for investors:** SaaS acquires the customer cheaply; payments and
  lending monetize them deeply; the dataset makes each layer defensible.

> **The one-sentence roadmap for the deck:** *"Land as the cheapest, easiest
> ERP for a contractor; expand into the payments and financing that every one
> of them needs - and that only we can underwrite, because we hold the
> ledger."*

## Accelerator-application angle (YC + Europe)
