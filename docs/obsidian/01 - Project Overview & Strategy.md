# 🎯 Project Overview & Business Strategy

Return to [[00 - Index MOC]]

## 📌 Executive Summary

**ICONA** is an all in one **Construction ERP + CRM** designed explicitly for **small and medium construction companies (SMEs)**. 

While enterprise construction giants use multi-million dollar software like Procore or Oracle Primavera, smaller contractors rely on disconnected tools: WhatsApp messages, Excel sheets, manual paper invoices, and informal cash payments. ICONA bridges this gap by offering a streamlined, modern software suite tailored to site engineers, project managers, financial supervisors, and real estate investors.

---

## 💡 The Core Problem & Solution

### The Challenge for Construction SMEs
1. **Cost Overruns & BOQ Leakage**: Inaccurate Bill of Quantities tracking leads to budget bleed on material purchases.
2. **Subcontractor & Labour Friction**: Tracking daily attendance, piece-rate wages, and milestone payouts across multiple sites causes major delays and disputes.
3. **Opaque Investor Relations**: Project funding investors lack real-time visibility into project progress and capital deployment.
4. **Site to Office Disconnect**: Site engineers struggle to report issues and material arrivals to office accountants in real time.

### The ICONA Solution
ICONA unifies operations into a single platform:
- **BOQ & Project Control**: Dynamic item estimation, progress reporting, and milestone gantt views.
- **Financial Control**: Cash flow forecasting, expense tracking, and vendor payout approvals.
- **Labour & Subcontractor Hub**: Daily site attendance logging, task assignments, and wage ledgering.
- **Investor Portal**: High level metrics, progress photo feeds, and draw down transparency.
- **Mobile Companion App**: Lightweight Android app for site engineers operating in low connectivity field settings.
- **AI Construction Assistant**: Telegram and web based assistant for instant query resolution and document parsing.

---

## 🎯 Target Market & Audience

- **Primary Persona**: General Contractors (GCs) managing projects from $100K to $10M total development value.
- **Secondary Persona**: Specialized Subcontractors (Civil, Electrical, HVAC, Finishing contractors).
- **Tertiary Persona**: Private Real Estate Investors and Project Lenders seeking capital verification.

## 💳 Business & Pricing Model

ICONA operates as a tiered B2B SaaS model:

| Tier | Target Scale | Key Capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 1 to 3 Active Projects | Core BOQ, Basic Cash Flow, Mobile Site App |
| **Growth** | 4 to 15 Active Projects | Advanced Subcontractor Ledger, Telegram Bot, Investor Updates |
| **Enterprise** | Multi-site / Custom | Custom Workflows, Dedicated Account Support, API Access |
