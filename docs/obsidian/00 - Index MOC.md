# 🏗️ ICONA Construction ERP/CRM - Project Map (MOC)

Welcome to the **Obsidian Map of Content** for the **ICONA** codebase and business strategy. ICONA is a specialized SaaS platform bringing enterprise ERP and CRM capabilities (BOQ, financial control, subcontractor management, labour tracking, investor portal, and AI assistants) to small and medium construction companies.
