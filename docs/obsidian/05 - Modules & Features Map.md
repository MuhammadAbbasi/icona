# 🧩 Modules & Features Map

Return to [[00 - Index MOC]]

The **ICONA Construction ERP/CRM** platform consists of seven specialized modules tailored to solve pain points in construction site operations, financial control, and stakeholder management.

---

## 🏗️ 1. Bill of Quantities (BOQ) & Estimations

- **Itemized Estimations**: Break down projects into structured BOQ line items (excavation, concrete, rebar, masonry, finishing).
- **Unit Rate Libraries**: Maintain localized material and labor rate databases for instant budgeting.
- **Variation Order Tracking**: Track scope changes and extra work items with client approval workflows.
- **Material Requisitions**: Generate purchase requests directly tied to BOQ thresholds to eliminate waste.

---

## ⏱️ 2. Project Control & Milestone Management

- **Gantt & Phase Schedules**: Interactive timeline tracking planned vs. actual progress.
- **Site Daily Logs**: Photo updates, weather conditions, equipment usage, and safety audit logging.
- **Critical Path Alerts**: Instant notifications when critical path milestones fall behind schedule.

---

## 💰 3. Finance & Cash Flow Control

- **Cash Flow Forecasting**: Real time visibility into incoming client draw-downs versus outgoing supplier invoices.
- **Petty Cash & Site Expense Management**: Capture receipts on mobile and route to accountants for fast reconciliation.
- **Vendor & Supplier Ledger**: Manage invoices, retainage percentages, and payment schedules.

---

## 👷 4. Labour & Subcontractor Hub

- **Subcontractor Milestone Payments**: Tie subcontractor payouts directly to verified BOQ completion percentages.
- **Daily Attendance & Muster Roll**: Fast mobile check-in for daily wage laborers and specialized trades.
- **Piece Rate & Daily Wage Calculator**: Automated wage calculation preventing dispute over work days or overtime.

---

## 📊 5. Investor & Client Portal

- **Capital Draw Down Transparency**: Real estate investors monitor capital deployment against verified progress.
- **Live Site Photo Feeds**: Geotagged, timestamped progress photos uploaded from the field.
- **Financial Executive Summary**: Simple high level charts illustrating project IRR, spend rates, and completion estimates.

---

## 📱 6. Mobile Companion App (Android)

- **Field Optimized UI**: High contrast, simplified interface for site engineers wearing safety gloves in sunlight.
- **Offline First Sync**: Log attendance, material receipts, and issues without cellular connectivity; syncs automatically when online.
- **Camera & Barcode Scanner**: Instant invoice photo uploads and material delivery receipt tags.

---

## 🤖 7. AI Construction Assistant

- **Voice to Text Site Logging**: Site supervisors dictate daily progress logs via voice notes processed into structured tasks.
- **BOQ Document Parsing**: Extract line items, units, and rates directly from PDF or Excel BOQ uploads.
- **Telegram Bot Workflow**: Query project status, log site alerts, or verify balance balances via Telegram.
